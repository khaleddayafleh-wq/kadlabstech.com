How we build

Three rules every KADlabs app follows.

Pay once, own it

Try it free. If you like it, one purchase unlocks everything, restores on a new phone, and never turns into a subscription.

Your data stays yours

No accounts, no analytics, no ad networks. What you do in the app is stored on your device and nowhere else.

Made for everyone

Screen readers name every shape, text scales up to 150%, motion and vibration can be turned down, and every control works by touch or keyboard.
